Conceptual instrument, not a forecast. The map is curated by hand: which places count as having had a peaceful revolution is contested, contestable, and editable at the top of the source. Each entry links to Wikipedia as a starting point — dig deeper from there. Peoples and de-facto or disputed polities that are not UN members (diamond markers) are included as first-class participants and counted — legitimacy here follows those present, not those recognized. Situations marked developing (flamingo pink) are unfolding now and are deliberately not counted until they cross the line. The United Kingdom has its own fractal page →. The 51% “Charter rewrite” is an act of imagination about participatory, citizen-driven governance, not a legal claim.
