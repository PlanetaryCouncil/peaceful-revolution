A nation is online when ≥51% of its regions are online. The same rule scales up: the world activates when ≥51% of nations are online.

This is a conceptual instrument, not a forecast. The roster reflects the 2022–2026 wave of popular uprisings and peaceful revolutions; the regions and years are editable seeds, not official records. What counts as a “revolution” is contested, contestable, and meant to be argued over — the data lives at the top of the source and is yours to rewrite. The 51% “Charter rewrite” is an act of imagination about participatory, citizen-driven governance, not a legal claim.
