# Peaceful Revolution — Activation Threshold

A conceptual, citizen-built activation board. Each nation that comes online marks a place a peaceful revolution has taken hold; at a 51% majority of nations the board declares the UN Charter "rewritten by those present."

**Live:** https://map.planetarycouncil.org

- `index.html` — the world board (fractal 51% threshold; click a nation to drill in)
- `uk.html` — the UK fractal, all 650 constituencies

The same 51% rule applies at every level: regions form a nation, nations form the world. Situations marked *developing* (flamingo pink) are unfolding now and are deliberately not counted toward the threshold until they cross it.

A conceptual instrument, not a forecast. The roster reflects the 2022-2026 uprising wave and is contested, contestable, and editable at the top of the source.
